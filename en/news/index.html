Initium³ laureate of the Brussels Balthazar 2026

At the Brussels Furniture Fair, our Olwen sofa takes 3rd place at the Brussels Balthazar 2026, in the « Love at first sight » category.

A wonderful recognition for the whole team, and for what we stand for: hybrid furniture, as comfortable outdoors as it is indoors.

Thank you to the jury, to the fair organisers and to everyone who came to discover our pieces.
